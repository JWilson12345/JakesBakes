Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Jake's Bakes operates this store, website, and related ordering services, including the information, content, features, tools, products, and services made available to customers (together, the "Services"). Jake's Bakes is a small home-based bakery in Barnsley, South Yorkshire. We offer weekly, made-to-order Cookies for collection in Barnsley. Our public website is currently provided through GitHub Pages, and our online ordering, checkout, payment, and related ecommerce functions are powered by Shopify.

This Privacy Policy explains how Jake's Bakes ("Jake's Bakes," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses your personal information when you visit or use the Services, place or collect an order, submit a review, contact us, or otherwise communicate with us. If there is a conflict between our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, this Privacy Policy controls in relation to the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of personal information.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood how your information is handled as described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by law, such as for certain non-essential cookies or marketing communications, we will ask for your consent separately.

Personal Information We Collect or Process

When we use the term "personal information," we mean information that identifies you or can reasonably be linked to you or another person. It does not include information that has been collected anonymously or properly de-identified so that it cannot reasonably identify or be linked to you.

Depending on how you interact with Jake's Bakes, where you live, the features you use, and what is permitted or required by law, we may collect or process the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact details, including your name, email address, telephone number, billing address, any other address you choose to provide, and the name used to collect an order.
  • Order and collection information, including your order number, selected products, bag size, quantity, price, payment status, order date, chosen collection date or time slot, collection status, changes, cancellations, refunds, complaints, and previous transactions.
  • Financial and payment information, including your payment method, transaction details, payment confirmation, billing details, and limited payment information supplied to us by Shopify or the relevant payment provider. Full payment-card details are generally collected and processed directly by Shopify and its payment providers and are not normally visible to Jake's Bakes.
  • Account information, if you choose to use an optional Shopify, Shop, Shop Pay, or customer-account feature, including your username, login details, preferences, settings, saved information, and order history. A Jake's Bakes account is not currently required to place an order, and guest checkout may be available.
  • Communications with us, including information contained in emails, contact-form submissions, social-media messages, customer-support requests, complaints, and other communications.
  • Review and feedback information, if you submit a review or testimonial, including your name or chosen display name, rating, review text, order-verification information, photographs you provide, and your permission to publish the review. We will only publish customer feedback with permission.
  • Device and technical information, including your device type, browser, operating system, network connection, IP address, cookie identifiers, and other unique or technical identifiers.
  • Usage information, including how and when you visit, interact with, or navigate the Services, the pages or products you view, links you select, cart activity, and information about errors or performance.
  • Allergy, dietary, or health-related information that you voluntarily provide when asking a question or discussing an order. Such information may be sensitive personal information. We will use it only as reasonably necessary to respond to you, assess whether we can safely meet a request, manage the order, comply with legal obligations, or protect someone's vital interests. Providing this information does not guarantee that we can accommodate an allergy or dietary requirement, and customers must review the current ingredient and allergen information before ordering.

We do not intentionally request information that is not reasonably relevant to operating Jake's Bakes. Please do not send unnecessary confidential information through unsecured channels.

Personal Information Sources

We may collect personal information from the following sources:

  • Directly from you, including when you browse the Services, place an order, select a collection slot, make a payment, contact us, submit a review, subscribe to marketing, or otherwise provide information.
  • Automatically through the Services, including from your browser or device and through cookies, pixels, local storage, server logs, and similar technologies.
  • From Shopify and other service providers, including when they provide ecommerce, checkout, payment, fraud-prevention, hosting, analytics, communication, customer-support, storage, or related services for us.
  • From payment providers, where they provide payment status, fraud checks, chargeback information, or transaction details.
  • From social-media platforms or other third parties, when you interact with our social pages, follow a link, use an integration, or otherwise direct a third party to provide information to us.
  • From publicly available sources, where permitted by law and reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Depending on how you interact with us and which Services you use, we may use personal information for the following purposes:

We may also measure the effectiveness of our promotions and, where enabled and legally permitted, use advertising or audience tools supplied by Shopify or other partners. Lawful Bases for Processing

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform our contract with you, such as taking payment, preparing your order, arranging collection, and providing order support.
  • Legal obligation: processing is necessary to comply with laws that apply to us, including tax, accounting, consumer-protection, food-safety, and data-protection requirements.
  • Legitimate interests: processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests or those of a third party, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights. These interests may include operating and improving Jake's Bakes, managing customer relationships, securing the Services, preventing fraud, keeping appropriate business records, and handling or defending claims.
  • Consent: you have given consent for a particular purpose, such as receiving optional marketing, allowing non-essential cookies, publishing a review, or, where required, processing sensitive information that you voluntarily provide. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
  • Vital interests: in rare circumstances, processing may be necessary to protect someone's life or physical safety, including in an allergy-related emergency.

  • Right to restrict processing: the right to ask us to limit how we use personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: the right, in certain circumstances, to receive personal information you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to ask for it to be transferred to another organisation.
