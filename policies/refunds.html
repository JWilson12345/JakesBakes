Policy

Return and Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Overview

Jake's Bakes makes fresh, perishable food products in small batches for weekly collection. For that reason, we do not offer a general 30-day return or change-of-mind policy. Food cannot normally be returned for resale, and the usual cooling-off right for many online purchases generally does not apply to goods that are liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights. If a product is faulty, unsafe, not as described, not of satisfactory quality, unfit for an expressly agreed purpose, missing, damaged or different from what you ordered, you may be entitled to a replacement, full or partial refund, or another appropriate remedy.

This Return and Refund Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, product descriptions, ingredient and allergen information and collection instructions.

Order Changes and Cancellations

Please check your products, quantities, collection date, collection time, contact details and allergen information carefully before placing your order.

To request a change or cancellation, contact us as soon as possible at jakesbakesuk@outlook.com or 07539 155591 and include your name and order number.

Because each weekly batch is limited and products are prepared for a specific collection date, changes and cancellations are not guaranteed after an order has been accepted, ingredients have been allocated or preparation has begun.

Where we accept a cancellation before preparation begins, we will normally issue a full refund to the original payment method. Where only part of an order can be cancelled, we may refund the cancelled part. Nothing in this section limits any cancellation or refund right that applies by law.

Returns of Food Products

We do not accept physical returns of cookies or other food products merely because you have changed your mind, ordered the wrong flavour or quantity or no longer want the order.

For food-safety reasons, returned food cannot be resold. Do not post, deliver or bring a product back to the collection address unless we have asked you to do so. We do not provide return shipping labels because Jake's Bakes is currently a collection-only bakery.

This does not prevent you from reporting a faulty, unsafe, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed product or from receiving any remedy to which you are legally entitled.

Damaged, Incorrect, Faulty or Unsafe Products

Please inspect your order when it is collected and contact us promptly if:

  • You receive the wrong product or quantity.
  • An item is missing.
  • The product or packaging is materially damaged.
  • The product is stale, spoiled, contaminated or otherwise unsafe.
  • Required ingredient or allergen information appears to be missing or incorrect.
  • The product is materially different from its description.
  • There is another quality problem that was present when the order was collected.

Where reasonably possible, contact us within 24 hours of collection. This helps us investigate a fresh-food complaint, but it does not replace or shorten any statutory right you may have.

Please provide your name, order number, a clear explanation of the issue and photographs of the product, packaging and label where relevant. We may also ask you to keep the affected product and packaging for a reasonable period so that we can investigate. Do not keep or transport food where doing so would be unsafe.

If you believe there is an allergen or food-safety problem, do not consume the product. If anyone experiences a serious allergic reaction or medical emergency, seek urgent medical assistance before contacting us.

How We Will Put a Problem Right

After reviewing the information available, we may offer one or more of the following, as appropriate:

  • A replacement or remake, subject to safety and production capacity.
  • A refund for the affected product.
  • A partial refund where only part of the order is affected.
  • A full refund where the whole order is affected.
  • Another remedy agreed with you.

Any replacement collection date or time must be agreed with us. If a replacement is not possible within a reasonable period, we may provide a refund instead.

We may decline a voluntary refund where the problem results from normal handmade variation, personal taste, failure to follow storage or reheating instructions, damage after collection, consumption after the recommended period or information that was accurately disclosed before purchase.

We will not use this provision to deny a statutory remedy where the product did not conform to the contract.

Handmade Variations

Jake's Bakes products are handmade. Reasonable differences in shape, size, colour, spread, decoration, texture or the distribution of fillings and toppings are normal and are not, by themselves, faults.

Product photographs are illustrative. Screen settings, lighting, seasonal ingredients and small-batch preparation may also cause minor differences. A product will still be expected to match its material description and meet applicable quality and safety standards.

Allergies and Dietary Requirements

Customers must review the current ingredient and allergen information before ordering and when collecting the product. Contact us before ordering if the intended consumer has an allergy, intolerance, coeliac disease or other dietary requirement.

We may be unable to offer a refund where accurate allergen information and cross-contamination warnings were provided and the issue arose because that information was not reviewed or a relevant requirement was not disclosed.

This does not limit our responsibility for inaccurate or legally inadequate allergen information, unsafe food, negligence or any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Missed or Late Collections

Orders must be collected from the agreed address during the confirmed collection date and time slot. Contact us promptly if you expect to be late.

Because products are freshly prepared and perishable, we cannot guarantee that an order will remain available or at its best after the agreed collection period.

A missed collection does not automatically qualify for a refund or free remake where the order was prepared correctly and made available as agreed.

We will consider exceptional circumstances fairly. This section does not apply where the collection failure was caused by Jake's Bakes.

Exchanges

We do not offer ordinary exchanges for change-of-mind requests. Because our products are food and are made for limited weekly collections, an unwanted product cannot be returned and exchanged for another flavour or bag.

Where a product is faulty, incorrect, unsafe or not as described, we may offer a replacement or remake as a legal or voluntary remedy. Otherwise, a different product must be purchased through a new order, subject to availability.

Sale Items, Discounts and Promotions

Sale, discounted and promotional products have the same statutory quality and refund rights as full-price products.

We do not provide change-of-mind refunds merely because a sale or promotional product is no longer wanted. Promotion-specific conditions may apply, but they will not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Gift Cards

Where Jake's Bakes offers gift cards or store credit, they are not exchangeable for cash and are not refundable merely because the purchaser or recipient changes their mind, except where required by law.

If a refund is due for an order paid wholly or partly using a gift card or store credit, the relevant amount may be returned to the same gift card or form of store credit unless the law requires another method.

Cooling-Off Periods

The standard 14-day cooling-off right for many distance contracts does not generally apply to goods that are liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly, such as freshly prepared food. It may also not apply to products made to a customer's specifications or clearly personalised.

Jake's Bakes therefore does not offer a 14-day or 30-day change-of-mind return period for collected cookies or other perishable food products.

This exception does not affect your rights when products are faulty, unsafe, damaged, incorrect, not as described or otherwise do not meet the requirements of consumer law.

Refunds

We will notify you when we have reviewed your request and tell you whether a refund has been approved and what amount will be refunded.

Approved refunds will normally be issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. We may use another method where you agree or where the original method is no longer available and an alternative is lawful.

Banks, card providers, Shopify and other payment providers may take additional time to process and display a refund after we have issued it.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we confirmed that your refund was approved, please contact us.

We will not charge an administration fee for a refund that you are legally entitled to receive.

Proof of Purchase and Fraud Prevention

We may ask for reasonable proof that the order was purchased from Jake's Bakes, such as an order confirmation, order number, receipt, payment record or the email address used at checkout.

We may verify the identity of the purchaser before issuing a refund and may investigate requests that appear fraudulent, duplicated, abusive or connected with an unauthorised payment.

These checks will not be used to unreasonably delay or refuse a genuine statutory claim.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first so that we have a reasonable opportunity to investigate and resolve the issue. Nothing in this policy prevents you from contacting your card issuer or exercising any chargeback or payment-dispute right available to you.

Knowingly making a false chargeback or claiming that an order was not collected when it was collected may constitute fraud and may result in evidence being provided to the payment provider or relevant authorities.
