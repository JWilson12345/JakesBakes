Policy

Accessibility Statement

Jake's Bakes is committed to making its website clear, accessible and easy to use for as many people as possible.

We aim to provide readable text, straightforward navigation, labelled forms, visible keyboard focus and a mobile-friendly layout. We also continue to review the website and make improvements where possible.

Accessibility Feedback

If you experience difficulty using any part of the website, please contact Jake and include the page name and a brief description of the problem. We will review the issue and do our best to provide the information or assistance you need.
