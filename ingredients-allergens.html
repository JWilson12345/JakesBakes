Ingredients and Allergens

Clear information before you order.

Allergy advice: for allergens, see ingredients in bold. Jake's Bakes is a small home-baking business. If you have an allergy or intolerance, contact Jake before ordering so the current batch ingredients can be checked with you.

The Biscoff Royale

Jake's signature cookie: browned British butter, white and milk chocolate, Biscoff biscuit and a smooth, milky Nutella drizzle, made with 48-hour rested dough and baked with a crisp outside and soft centre.

Ingredients

Wheat Flour (Plain Flour, Strong White Bread Flour), Salted Butter (Milk), Dark Brown Soft Sugar, Granulated Sugar, White Chocolate (Milk, Soya), Milk Chocolate (Milk, Soya), Milk Chocolate Chips (Milk, Soya), Free Range Egg, Madagascan Vanilla Extract, Raising Agent (Sodium Bicarbonate), Salt, Biscoff biscuit crumb (Wheat / gluten) and milky Nutella drizzle (Hazelnuts, Milk).

Allergens

  • Wheat / gluten
  • Milk
  • Egg
  • Soya
  • Hazelnuts

May contain peanuts and tree nuts depending on ingredient packaging.

Box information

  • 4 Cookies: 4 x approx. 80g cookies.
  • 6 Cookies: 6 x approx. 60g cookies.
  • Handmade in small batches in Barnsley.
  • Collection only.

Appearance, weight and texture can vary slightly because every cookie is made by hand.

Dietary and storage information

  • Suitable for vegetarians.
  • Not vegan, dairy free or gluten free.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep in an airtight container after collection.
  • Best enjoyed within 3 days, suitable for up to 5 days when stored correctly.
